## Security and authentication

Use the following features from your Agora account to implement security and authentication features in your apps.

### Generate temporary tokens

To ensure communication security, best practice is to use tokens to authenticate users who log in from your client app.

To generate a temporary RTC + RTM token for your project:

1. Select your project from the dropdown at the top of the page.
1. Click **Project Settings** in the sidebar.
1. On the **Credentials** panel, click **Generate Temp Token**
1. Enter a channel name and **RTC UID** in the pop-up box and click **Generate token**.
1. Copy the generated token.
