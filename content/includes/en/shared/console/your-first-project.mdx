### Your first Agora project

When you create a new account, Agora creates a default project and opens it for you when you log in.

To create a new project:

1. In [Agora Console](https://console.agora.io/v2), open the [All Projects](https://console.agora.io/projects) page.
2. Click **Create New Project**.
3. Follow the on-screen instructions to enter a **Project Name** and select a **Hosting Region**. Keep secure token authentication enabled to secure your project.

    ![Create a new project](/console-tmp/create-new-project.png)

4. Click **Create Project**. You see the new project on the **All Projects** page.
