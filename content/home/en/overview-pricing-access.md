## Ground rules

### Prepare access first

Enable the service and collect App ID plus credentials before meaningful integration work begins.

### Map entitlements by capability

Different capability domains may require different enablement states. Readers should understand this before they reach implementation blockers.

### Split credential responsibilities

Separate server secrets, client identity, and temporary credentials before exposing the platform to external users or devices.
