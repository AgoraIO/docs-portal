## What this capability is

Smart-device scenarios focus on realtime coordination among devices, terminals, and cloud systems, especially for cameras, doorbells, watches, and IoT endpoints.

## Common scenarios

- cameras
- doorbells
- watches
- terminal assistants

## What to read first

If the main problem is "how does the device join the realtime path," start from device-oriented scenarios before deciding whether RTC, RTSA, or messaging is the dominant layer.
