## What Agora provides

Agora gives teams a realtime foundation for conversational AI, voice, video, messaging, and media services.

## Core concepts

- Projects and credentials define the account boundary for integrations.
- Channels, users, agents, and events define the live session model.
- Media, messaging, and backend operations work together instead of living in isolated product silos.
