## What this capability solves

Realtime Messaging RTM provides low-latency, high-concurrency, scalable, and reliable messaging plus state synchronization. It is the right layer for channel messages, presence, business events, and collaborative realtime workflows.

## Common scenarios

- synchronizing state between AI agents and clients
- channel messaging and collaboration events
- user presence, temporary state, and business metadata
- education, live interaction, smart devices, and collaborative products

## Why it belongs in the new capability area

RTM should not appear only as a supporting feature beneath AI. It is a standalone capability domain that underpins realtime products, agent coordination, and business workflow synchronization.
