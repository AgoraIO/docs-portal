## What this capability is

RTMP Gateway solves the protocol-bridging problem between live interaction systems and standard RTMP-based streaming workflows.

## Common scenarios

- protocol conversion
- live-chain interop
- connection to RTMP-based ecosystems

## What to read first

First confirm whether the problem is protocol interop or a broader live distribution problem. The latter usually leads further into Fusion CDN.
