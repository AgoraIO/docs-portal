# Product Overview

Agora Conversational AI Engine redefines the human-computer interaction interface by moving beyond traditional text interaction and enabling highly realistic, natural, and fluid real-time voice conversations, so AI can truly speak. It supports personalized voice customization, emotional expression, and deep multi-turn conversation understanding, giving AI agents stronger expressiveness and richer interaction. It is suitable for innovative scenarios such as intelligent assistants, emotional companionship, spoken-language practice, intelligent customer service, smart devices, and immersive game NPCs, allowing AI agents to build more natural and vivid interactions with users through a warmer and more expressive voice.

![Conversational AI Engine Technical Architecture](https://doc.shengwang.cn/img/convoai/tech-arch.png)

## Product Advantages

Agora Conversational AI Engine offers the following advantages:

- **Ultra-low latency**: End-to-end response latency can be as low as 650 ms thanks to deep full-link optimization, ensuring smooth and natural conversations.
- **Selective attention locking**: Filters out 95% of background voices and noise to accurately identify the target speaker.
- **Graceful interruption**: Delivers interruption response in as little as 340 ms, simulating the rhythm of real human conversation and making interactions feel more natural.
- **Packet-loss resilience**: Maintains stable and smooth conversations even with 80% packet loss, and can still respond smoothly after 3 to 5 seconds of network interruption.
- **Flexible extensibility**: Compatible with mainstream global LLM and TTS providers to meet the needs of different scenarios and businesses.
- **Fast integration**: Build a conversational AI agent quickly with 2 lines of code and 15 minutes of work.

## Supported Capabilities

With AI and large-model capabilities, Agora Conversational AI Engine supports the following features in your projects:

| Capability | Description |
| --- | --- |
| AI real-time voice conversation | Users can talk with an AI agent by voice in a way that feels as natural as speaking with a real person. |
| Voice interruption | Users can interrupt the AI agent at any time and receive a fast response, improving conversational efficiency and fluency. |
| AI noise reduction | Intelligently filters environmental noise to improve interaction accuracy. |
| Background voice suppression | Effectively separates the target voice from background voices to keep conversations clear and free from interference. |
