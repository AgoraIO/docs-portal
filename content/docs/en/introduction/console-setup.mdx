Use Agora Console to manage your projects and account settings. Console settings are organized into two groups:

- **Project settings**: Apply to the currently selected project.
- **Account settings**: Apply across your entire Agora account.

## Project level view

When you log in to Agora Console, you land on the project-level view for the selected project. Use the project dropdown at the top of the page to switch between projects. Agora creates a default project for you when you create a new account.

![Project Overview page](/console-tmp/project-overview.png)

### Overview 

The **Overview** page is the landing page for a project in Agora Console. It shows your project credentials (App ID and certificate), quick links to get started with agents, campaigns, and phone numbers, shortcuts to services, usage, and billing, and a summary of your plan quotas across RTC, Signaling, and Conversational AI.

### Project settings

The **Project Settings** page shows the basic identity and security configuration for the selected project.

- **Project Name**: An editable, human-readable name for the project. Select **Edit** to rename the project.

- **App ID**: A unique, non-editable identifier for the project. Select the copy icon to copy the ID to your clipboard.

- **Primary Certificate**: The project's primary server-side secret, used to generate authentication tokens. Enabling the primary certificate (default) secures the project and requires clients to authenticate with a token. Treat the primary certificate like a database password.

- **Secondary Certificate**: An optional fallback certificate used during certificate rotation. Enable it using the **Add a Certificate** button. You can't delete a secondary certificate while it is enabled; disable it first before deleting.

You can copy your **App ID** and **Primary certificate** individually or download your Agora `env` file for use in your projects.

![Primary and Secondary Certificate settings](/console-tmp/project-settings.png)

You can also disable your project from the **Project Settings** page.

### Rotate certificates

If you suspect your primary certificate has been compromised, rotate it using the secondary certificate. Replacing the primary certificate may impact your live business, so only rotate certificates when necessary.

1. Enable the secondary certificate.
2. Swap the primary and secondary certificates.
3. Disable the certificate now in the secondary slot (the original primary certificate).
4. Once users have switched to the new certificate, delete the disabled certificate.

:::warning
Once deleted, a certificate can't be restored. Before deleting it, confirm that users have switched to the new certificate. Deleting a certificate immediately invalidates all tokens generated with it, and users already connected can't renew their tokens using it.
:::

### Realtime Services

The **Realtime Services** page lists available Agora products grouped by category and lets you enable, disable, and configure each one.

#### Product list

The left panel groups products into the following categories:

- **Core RTC**: Chat, Signaling, Conversational AI Engine, Interactive Whiteboard.
- **Media Services**: Cloud Recording, Media Push, Media Pull, Media Gateway, Real-Time STT, Video Screenshot Upload, Notifications.
- **Security & Infrastructure**: Cloud Proxy, Co-Host Authentication.
- **Vertical Solutions**: Flexible Classroom.


#### Product configuration

Select a product to view and configure its settings in the panel on the right. Use the **Active** toggle at the top of the panel to enable or disable the product for the selected project. A checkmark next to a product name indicates it's enabled for the project. Refer to the specific product documentation for available configuration options.

![Realtime Services product list and configuration panel](/console-tmp/realtime-services.png)

### Analytics

Selecting **Analytics** in the left navigation opens Agora Analytics in a new tab. Use it to track and analyze usage and quality data for RTC and Chat.

## Account settings

Click the username at the bottom of the navigation sidebar to open the account menu popup. Items in this menu apply to your Agora account and not to any single project. From here, you can create and manage your projects, view billing status and invoices, see usage statistics, quotas, and breakdowns, open the Extensions Marketplace, and manage your profile and account-wide preferences and subscription plans.

The menu also gives you access to developer and support resources, including the Developer Hub, Help Hub, and notifications, as well as account-wide actions like switching to Light Mode, reverting to the legacy Console, and logging out.

Selecting any item under Account or Developer & Support opens the account-level view, which has its own persistent left navigation with the same items shown in the popup.

### View all plans

The **View all plans** page shows subscription plan options for Agora products and lets you upgrade or manage your current plan.

For detailed, up-to-date pricing for a specific product, see that product's pricing page.

### Billing

The **Billing** page shows your account balance, invoices, transactions, payment methods, and billing information.

- **Account Balance**: Your available balance, plus options to top up your wallet or withdraw funds once a payment method is on file.
- **Reserved Balance**: Funds reserved against estimated usage. Select **View Estimate** to see the breakdown.
- **Your invoices**: Monthly and pre-paid invoices, filterable by date range.
- **Transactions**: A log of balance movements, filterable by type, plus a separate tab for withdraw requests.
- **Payment methods**: Saved cards and bank transfer details used for billing.
- **Billing Information**: The name and address associated with your billing card.
- **Billing FAQ**: Information about billing, such as schedule, account suspension policy, payment orders, and refunds.

![Billing page showing balance, invoices, and transactions](/console-tmp/billing.png)

#### Payment methods

Manage saved cards and bank transfer details used for billing payments.

**Cards**

Select **Add New Card** to save a credit card for billing and invoice settlement. Provide the card number, name on card, expiration date, and CVC. Optionally set the card as the default for future payments.

![Add New Card dialog](/console-tmp/add-new-card.png)

**Bank Transfer**

Switch to the **Bank Transfer** tab for wire transfer instructions, including the beneficiary's name, account number, bank, SWIFT code, and bank address. Include the provided note (your Company ID) in the transfer's notes field.

:::warning
Transfers made without the provided note can't be matched to your account and will be disregarded.
:::

### Usage

The **Usage** page shows your account's usage for the current billing cycle.

- **Total Usage**: A summary of RTC minutes, agent minutes, and Signaling messages used in the current billing cycle.
- **Detailed Usage**: A breakdown of usage by product, filterable by project and date range. Select **View Breakdown** to chart usage over time, and export the data using the download icon.

![Detailed Usage chart with filters](/console-tmp/usage-detailed.png)

For deeper usage insights, use Agora Analytics.

### Extensions Marketplace

Use **Extensions Marketplace** to browse and manage extensions that add features to your app on top of Agora SDKs.

### Preferences

The **Preferences** page lets you manage your profile, notification settings, team members, and SSO integration.

#### Profile

Add or edit the following information:

- **Personal Information**: First name, last name, and display language.
- **Security**: Email, password, and phone number. Verify your phone number to enable it. Toggle **Enable Two-Factor Authentication** for added login security, or **Allow Agora Customer Support to login your Console account** to grant temporary support access for the next 24 hours.
- **Company Information**: Organisation name and Company ID.
- **Account Context**: The current account name and company country.
- **Danger Zone**: Delete your Agora account. This action is irreversible.

    :::caution
    Deleting your account permanently removes your account and project information, which can't be recovered. 
    :::

    Before you delete your account, ensure that:

    - You are the account creator, and this is an Agora Console account, not an [Agora Reseller Console](https://reseller.agora.io/) account.
    - All projects are archived and your account balance is zero.
    - The previous month's bill has been issued, no usage is generated in the current month, and all packages have expired or been used.
    - No other members remain under the account.

    To delete your account, in the **Danger Zone** panel on the **Profile** tab of the **Preferences** page, select **Delete Account**, type `delete my account` to confirm, and select **Delete Account** again.

#### Notifications

The **Notifications** tab lets you configure Agora alert preferences by notification type and delivery channel.

#### Members & Roles

The **Members & Roles** tab lets you manage who has access to your account.

- **Members**: Lists each member, their role, status, and any remarks. Select **Invite Member** to add a new member.
- **Roles**: Lists each role, its permissions, and the projects it applies to. Permissions cover Usage, Finance, Teams & Members, Projects, XLA, and Agora Analytics access. Agora provides five built-in roles: **Finance**, **Engineer**, **CS & Maintenance**, **Product & Operation**, and **Admin**. Built-in roles are locked and can't be edited.

![Roles table showing permissions by module](/console-tmp/members-roles.png)

Select **Create Role** to define a custom role. When creating a role, set:

- **Role name**: A name for the role.
- **Permissions**: Access level (**None**, **View**, or **Edit**, depending on the module) for Usage, Finance, Teams & Members, Projects, and XLA.
- **Agora Analytics**: Access to Call Inspector, Data Insights, and Live Data.
- **Data**: The projects the role applies to. Select **All Projects**, or choose specific projects.

![Create Role dialog with permissions form](/console-tmp/create-role.png)

#### SSO

The **SSO** tab lets you integrate Agora Console with Okta as your identity provider, so your team can log in using their Okta SSO credentials. This integration manages access using SAML and SCIM, and requires an active Premium or Enterprise Support Plan. Select **Request** to request the integration.

### View all Projects

The **View all Projects** page lists every project in your account, including its App ID, status, and last updated and created dates. Use the **Active** and **Archived** tabs to filter the list, and the search box to find a specific project by name. Select **Create New Project** to create a new project. Each account can create up to 20 projects; contact Agora support to request more.

Each project shows one of two status values:

- **Secure**: The project has a primary certificate enabled and requires authentication tokens.
- **Unsecure**: The project doesn't have a primary certificate enabled and doesn't require authentication tokens.

Select the actions menu at the end of a project's row to open, rename, or archive it.

![List all projects](/console-tmp/all-projects.png)

#### Create a project

Select **Create New Project** to create a new project with default Conversational AI and RTM setup.

- **Project Name**: A name for the new project.
- **Hosting Region**: The region used for the project's Conversational AI and RTM setup.
- **Primary Certificate**: Enabled by default, requiring secure token authentication for the project. Disable it to create the project without token authentication.

Select **Create Project** to finish.

![Create New Project dialog](/console-tmp/create-new-project.png)

### Developer Hub

The **Developer Hub** page has three tabs: **Overview**, **RESTful API**, and **SDK Toolkit**.

#### Overview

Choose a project from the dropdown to view its credentials and settings.

- **Project credentials**: Copy or download an Agora `.env` file containing the selected project's App ID and App Certificate, ready for local SDK setup.
- **Security**: View and manage the project's Primary and Secondary Certificates. See [Security](#security) for details on these certificates.
- **Project settings**: View and rename the project, and view or copy its App ID.

#### RESTful API

The **RESTful API** tab lists your API keys, used to authenticate with Agora's RESTful API.

Each key consists of a Customer ID and Customer Secret. Select **Add Secret** to generate a new key. You can download the Customer Secret only once, so save it somewhere secure. Add a note to a key to help identify it, and select the trash icon to delete a key. You can have a maximum of 10 keys per account.

![RESTful API keys](/console-tmp/restful-api-keys.png)

#### SDK Toolkit

The **SDK Toolkit** tab lists downloadable SDKs and sample apps.

- **Download SDKs**: Download or view the repository for each Agora SDK, filterable by platform.
- **Sample Apps**: Open sample apps demonstrating Agora products, filterable by platform.

### Help Hub

The **Help Hub** page has three tabs: **Overview**, **My Tickets**, and **What's New**.

#### Overview

- **Contact Support**: Select **Create a Support Ticket** to get help from Agora's support team.
- **Talk to our sales team**: Select **Talk to Sales** to discuss volume pricing or custom enterprise solutions.
- **Status page**: View current service health, or select **Go to Status Page** for maintenance updates.
- **Join our community**: Connect with other developers on Agora Discord or Stack Overflow.
- **Popular Articles**: Search or filter frequently asked questions by category, such as Integration Issues, Account & Billing, Product Inquiry, and Quality Issues.

#### My Tickets

- **My Tickets**: Select **View My Tickets** to view your support tickets and track the status of open requests.
- **Create a Ticket**: Select **Create a Ticket** to get help from Agora's support team.

#### What's New

- **Agora Blog**: Select **Go to Blog** to read product updates, customer stories, and engineering insights from the Agora team.
- **Release Notes**: Select **View Release Notes** to browse the latest Conversational AI and platform release notes on Agora Docs.

### Notifications

The **Notifications** page lists account notifications. Filter by category using **All**, **Unread**, **Account**, **Billing**, **Product**, or **System**. Select a notification to read its full content, or select **View in new tab** to open it separately. Select **Notification preferences** to manage which notifications you receive, or **Refresh** to check for new ones.
