Real-Time Speech to Text transcribes live audio streams into text, enabling closed captions, live transcription, and AI-powered workflows. Translate transcribed text into multiple languages in real time, or pass it directly to large language models to bridge real-time communication with intelligent applications.

## Common scenarios

- meeting captions and live subtitles
- multilingual live streaming and events
- voice understanding and AI preprocessing
- caption recording and synchronized playback

## Key capabilities

<CardGrid>
  <FeatureCard title="Live transcription for RTC">
    Integrated with Agora's voice and video service, live transcription and captions improve accessibility for meetings, live streaming, lectures, interviews, live shopping, and similar experiences.
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard title="Real-time translation (Beta)">
    Break down language barriers with live speech-to-text translation into multiple languages during realtime communication or live streaming, with high accuracy and ultra-low latency.
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard title="Cloud-based STT">
    A cloud-based service converts voice to text for active or selected hosts and distributes the text to all participants in the channel without depending on client device performance or network conditions.
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard title="Speaker labeling">
    Label each transcribed text segment with the speaker's UID. Separate transcription for each host helps maintain accuracy even when multiple hosts speak at the same time.
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard title="Caption recording">
    Upload transcriptions as `.vtt` files to cloud storage so recorded audio or video can be played back with synchronized closed captions.
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard title="Multi-language support">
    Realtime transcription supports major languages and dialects, while realtime translation supports 40+ target languages.
  </FeatureCard>
</CardGrid>
