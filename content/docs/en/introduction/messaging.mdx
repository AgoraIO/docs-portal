![Signaling overview](/images/signaling/signaling-overview.png)

Signaling is the messaging and state layer for realtime systems. It enables low-latency metadata synchronization and realtime event notifications, and complements RTC and AI flows with channel management, presence updates, attribute synchronization, and workflow coordination.

## Common scenarios

- channel messaging and pub/sub delivery
- presence and contact availability
- metadata and attribute synchronization
- business event coordination and realtime workflows

## Key capabilities

<CardGrid>
  <FeatureCard title="Message channels">
    Realtime messaging for asynchronous pub/sub transmission without requiring an immediate response. Publishers send messages to channels, and subscribers receive messages from the channels they join, using either string or binary payloads.
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard title="Stream channels">
    A realtime data pipeline that enables uninterrupted data flow from one point to another with minimal delay, supporting string or binary payloads based on your business needs.
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard title="Topics">
    Manage communication inside stream channels by letting users subscribe to, distribute, and notify others through topics. Publishers send messages to a topic, and subscribers receive messages from the topics they join.
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard title="Presence">
    Help users see the availability of their contacts in collaboration scenarios through presence status and user state, making communication and coordination easier.
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard title="Storage">
    Persist and manage data exchanged between clients or devices in realtime, helping ensure messages are not lost in transit and can be delivered reliably at scale.
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard title="Locks">
    Control access to shared resources, preserve processing order, and prevent concurrent interference when multiple clients interact with the same data.
  </FeatureCard>
</CardGrid>
