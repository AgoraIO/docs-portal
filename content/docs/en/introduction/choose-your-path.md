## Why this page exists

Agora spans AI, realtime engagement, messaging, and media workflows. New readers often know the outcome they want, but not the product boundary they need to enter first.

This page routes readers from user goals into the most appropriate capability domain, then points them toward the next technical layer.

## Recommended paths

### Build an AI voice app

Start here if you need realtime voice interaction, model orchestration, interruption control, and memory in the same product loop.

- [Open AI docs](/en/ai)
- [Open Conversational AI Engine quickstart](/en/ai/quick-start)

### Build realtime audio and video

Start here if low-latency audio, channel transport, voice presence, and media delivery are the core of the product.

- [Open Realtime & Media](/en/realtime-media)

### Integrate messaging and collaboration

Start here if state synchronization, events, callbacks, room collaboration, or business control flows matter more than the media layer.

- [Open event listening guidance](/en/ai/listen-agent-events)

### Use media processing services

Start here if recording, transcription, streaming IO, or downstream media handling is a critical part of the workflow.

- [Open audio modality guidance](/en/realtime-media/audio-modality)

## How to choose

Pick the domain that is closest to the problem you are solving now, not the full architecture you may build later.

If the first decision is wrong, the rest of the reading path becomes noisy very quickly. It is better to choose one strong entry point, complete the first loop, then expand into adjacent domains.
