## What you can build with Agora

- Voice assistants, copilots, tutors, and customer service agents built with **Conversational AI Engine**
- One-to-one calls, group video sessions, meetings, classrooms, and broadcast-style experiences built with **Video Calling** and **Broadcast Streaming**
- Channel messaging, presence, topics, storage, and metadata synchronization built with **Signaling**
- Live captions, translation, and speech understanding built with **Real-Time Speech to Text**
- Recording, transcoding, whiteboard collaboration, and analytics layered on top of live sessions

## Capability overview

### Conversational AI

[Conversational AI](/en/introduction/ai-agents) combines Agora's realtime audio streaming with the LLM of your choice. Key capabilities include:

- ultra-low latency
- customizable agent properties
- custom LLM support
- advanced audio features
- weak-network resilience
- multi-platform support

If your first goal is a working voice agent, start with [Start with AI](/en/introduction/start-with-ai), then continue to [AI overview](/en/ai) and [AI quickstart](/en/ai/get-started/quickstart).

### Audio & Video

[Audio & Video](/en/introduction/realtime-audio-video) is the entry point for realtime sessions built on Agora Video Calling. Key capabilities include:

- global coverage
- high-quality video at scale
- multiple audio and video tracks
- screen sharing and collaboration
- AI-powered audio enhancement
- call recording

### Messaging

[Messaging](/en/introduction/messaging) covers:

- message channels
- stream channels
- topics
- presence
- storage
- locks

If you need RTM-style coordination, continue to [Realtime Messaging RTM](/en/introduction/rtm) and the deeper [RTM product docs](/en/realtime-media/rtm).

### Transcription & Translation

[Transcription & Translation](/en/introduction/speech-to-text) covers:

- live transcription for RTC
- real-time translation
- cloud-based STT
- speaker labeling
- caption recording
- multi-language support

### Media infrastructure

Backend and device-side capabilities include:

- [Device Media Transport](/en/introduction/rtsa): IoT SDK for low-resource and battery-powered devices
- [Server Media Processing](/en/introduction/rtc-server-sdk): Server Gateway for backend send and receive flows
- [Live Streaming Distribution](/en/introduction/fusion-cdn): Broadcast Streaming for host-audience delivery
- [PPT Conversion](/en/introduction/ppt-transcoding): Cloud Transcoding for transcoding, audio mixing, and video compositing

### Collaboration and media services

Additional services extend the live session:

- [Collaborative Whiteboard](/en/introduction/whiteboard): editing tools, file conversion, SDK options, and room management APIs
- [Cloud/Local Recording](/en/introduction/recording): Cloud Recording and On-Premise Recording for storing live sessions

## Account and project setup

The shared account-management source treats Agora Console as the unified portal for configuring, purchasing, and managing products.

Use these pages to prepare access before implementation:

- [Account](/en/introduction/account): sign up, create a project, get the App ID and App Certificate, and generate temporary tokens
- [Projects](/en/introduction/projects): create projects, manage project details, and manage primary and secondary certificates
- [Members and Roles](/en/introduction/members-roles): add members, assign teams, and define custom permissions
- [Pricing & Access](/en/introduction/pricing-access): review service access requirements, product pricing models, and billing operations

## Suggested reading order

1. Read [About Agora](/en/introduction/about-agora) for the cross-product platform view.
2. If your product is AI-first, continue to [Start with AI](/en/introduction/start-with-ai).
3. If your product is session-first, continue to [Audio & Video](/en/introduction/realtime-audio-video) or [Messaging](/en/introduction/messaging).
4. Add [Transcription & Translation](/en/introduction/speech-to-text), [Collaborative Whiteboard](/en/introduction/whiteboard), or [Cloud/Local Recording](/en/introduction/recording) as your product surface expands.
