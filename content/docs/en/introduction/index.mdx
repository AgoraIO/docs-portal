Agora can look broad when you first land in the docs. The fastest way to get value is to avoid reading everything in order and instead choose the path that matches your first build milestone.

## Start here

- Use [Start with AI](/en/introduction/get-started/start-with-ai) if you want an AI-assisted workflow with MCP, Skills, CLI guidance, and official starters.
- Open the [Voice AI quickstart](/en/ai/choose-your-path/quickstart-coding) if your goal is to get a working voice agent running as quickly as possible.
- Open the [Voice & Video quickstart](/en/realtime-media/rtc/android/quick-start/build-from-scratch) if your first milestone is a live calling or meeting experience.

## Understand the platform

- [About Agora](/en/introduction/about-agora): what Agora is, what problems it solves, how the product families fit together, and where to start
- [Core Concepts](/en/introduction/core-concepts): shared platform primitives such as sessions, channels, credentials, and realtime building blocks

## Explore by capability

- [Conversational AI](/en/introduction/conversational-ai): voice agents, assistants, tutors, support agents, and spoken AI experiences
- [Real-Time Voice & Video](/en/introduction/realtime-audio-video): calling, meetings, classrooms, livestream interaction, and device media transport
- [Messaging & Presence](/en/introduction/messaging-presence): chat, signaling, room state, metadata, and collaborative coordination
- [Cloud Media Services](/en/introduction/cloud-media-services): recording, speech-to-text, translation, media routing, and stream transformation

## Production basics

- [Projects](/en/introduction/projects): project creation, service enablement, and certificate management
- [Console setup](/en/introduction/console-setup): credentials, console workflows, and first-time service setup
- [Pricing & Access](/en/introduction/pricing-access): service enablement and billing boundaries
- [Security and privacy](/en/introduction/security-privacy): security responsibilities and data handling
