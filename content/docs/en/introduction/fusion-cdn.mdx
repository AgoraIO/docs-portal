Fusion CDN Live Streaming is the large-scale live distribution path for host-audience products. It combines Agora's live streaming foundation with stronger CDN orchestration so teams can improve playback quality, distribution resilience, and audience reach across large live events.

## Common scenarios

- social live streaming and creator events
- entertainment, commerce, and showroom broadcasts
- broadcast-style delivery with large audiences
- multi-CDN playback optimization and resilient live distribution

## Key capabilities

<CardGrid>
  <FeatureCard title="Global coverage">
    Agora's Software-Defined Real-Time Network (SDRTN) supports live video delivery to users in over 200 countries and regions.
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard title="High-quality video at scale">
    Deliver consistent high-quality video from smaller interactive sessions to very large concurrent live audiences, even under challenging network conditions.
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard title="Multiple audio and video tracks">
    Publish multiple audio and video tracks to one or more channels from a single instance, with support for multi-channel capture cameras and microphones.
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard title="Screen sharing and collaboration">
    Support screen sharing and interactive presentation workflows that let hosts draw, annotate, and share content across devices during live sessions.
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard title="AI-powered audio enhancement">
    Improve the live listening experience with 3D spatial audio, AI noise suppression, and gain control.
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard title="Recording">
    Record live audio and video sessions in the cloud or on premises with control over format, storage path, and quality.
  </FeatureCard>
</CardGrid>

## What to read first

Start here when you already have a live pipeline and need stronger distribution orchestration. If you only need a basic push path, begin with Media Push before moving into Fusion CDN.
