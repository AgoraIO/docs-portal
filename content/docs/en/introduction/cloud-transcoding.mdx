title: Cloud Transcoding
description: Transcode realtime audio and video streams for audience subscription.

![Cloud Transcoding overview](/images/cloud-transcoding/cloud-transcoding.png)

Cloud Transcoding obtains audio and video source streams from RTC channels on the server side, processes them through transcoding, audio mixing, and video compositing, and then republishes the processed streams for audience subscription. It is designed for realtime interactive live broadcast scenarios where viewers need lower downstream bandwidth pressure and lower client performance requirements.

## Key capabilities

<CardGrid>
  <FeatureCard title="Low energy consumption for client devices">
    Reduce terminal performance consumption by processing streams server-side before publishing them back to the RTC channel, which is especially useful for viewer devices that cannot handle multiple HD streams at once.
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard title="Low bitrate streaming for poor network conditions">
    Improve fluency and viewing quality under weak downlink conditions by mixing audio, combining images, and transcoding them into lower bitrate output streams.
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard title="Cross-platform compatibility">
    Support standard H.264 and VP8 encoding, including conversion from Native host streams to VP8 for a better Web viewing experience.
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard title="Automated stream processing workflow">
    Automatically subscribe to configured user streams in RTC channels, perform transcoding, audio mixing, and video compositing, and publish the processed results back to the channel.
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard title="Reduced bandwidth and simplified integration">
    Reduce downstream bandwidth pressure by removing the need for viewers to subscribe to multiple host streams individually, while lowering integration complexity through scenario-based workflows.
  </FeatureCard>
</CardGrid>
