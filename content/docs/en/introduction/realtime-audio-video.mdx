RTC is the foundation for voice and video calling, interactive live streaming, meetings, and classrooms. It powers crystal-clear realtime voice communication and ultra-low-latency high-definition video across devices and platforms, with advanced codecs, network optimization, and dynamic adaptation for challenging network conditions.

## Common scenarios

- one-to-one and group voice and video calling
- interactive live streaming and social engagement
- online meetings and team collaboration
- interactive classrooms, telehealth, and connected-device experiences

## Key capabilities

<CardGrid>
  <FeatureCard title="Global coverage">
    Agora's Software-Defined Real-Time Network (SDRTN) supports voice and video users in over 200 countries and regions.
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard title="High-quality video at scale">
    Consistent high-quality video from 1:1 calls to thousands of concurrent users, even under challenging network conditions.
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard title="Multiple audio and video tracks">
    Publish multiple audio and video tracks to one or more channels from a single instance, with support for multi-channel capture cameras and microphones.
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard title="Screen sharing and collaboration">
    Enable screen sharing and interactive whiteboards that let users draw, annotate, and share content from multiple devices simultaneously.
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard title="AI-powered audio enhancement">
    Support 3D spatial audio, AI noise suppression, active speaker recognition, and gain control to provide an immersive audio experience.
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard title="Recording">
    Record live audio and video calls in the cloud or on premises with control over format, storage path, and quality.
  </FeatureCard>
