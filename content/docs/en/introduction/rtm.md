![Signaling overview](/images/signaling/signaling-overview.png)

This capability provides channel messaging, stream channels, topics, presence, storage, and synchronization between participants.

## What it is good at

- Channel and peer-style message delivery.
- Shared presence and temporary state.
- Topic-based coordination inside larger realtime systems.
- Metadata storage and locking for synchronized workflows.
