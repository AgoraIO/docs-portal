## What this capability is

Console acts as the control-plane entry for service enablement, project configuration, credentials, and resource administration across Agora products.

## Common scenarios

- enabling services
- retrieving credentials
- configuring projects
- managing resources

## What to read first

When you have not yet entered implementation, Console is usually the first operational step.
