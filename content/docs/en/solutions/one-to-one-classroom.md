## What this solution is

One-to-one Classroom is the education solution path for private tutoring style products. It is aimed at teams that want to build a customized teaching workflow around a single teacher and a single student.

## Best fit

- private tutoring
- language coaching
- music practice or other high-attention teaching formats

## Built on

- realtime audio and video
- teaching-side coordination and messaging
- whiteboard and class review workflows

## Choose this when

Choose this path when you need a highly tailored teaching product and the class model is fundamentally one teacher to one student.
