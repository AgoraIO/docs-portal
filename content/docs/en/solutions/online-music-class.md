## What this solution is

Online Music Class is a specialized education solution for music teaching and practice. It is designed for teams that need higher audio fidelity, music-oriented noise handling, and lesson interaction that works better for instruments and vocal training.

## Best fit

- one-to-one music lessons
- one-to-many music teaching
- accompaniment and practice coaching
- products where sound quality is more important than a generic classroom baseline

## Built on

- high-fidelity realtime audio and video
- classroom coordination and whiteboard support
- music-oriented audio tuning and teaching-side visual aids

## Choose this when

Choose Online Music Class when accurate sound reproduction and instrument-friendly interaction are central to the product.
