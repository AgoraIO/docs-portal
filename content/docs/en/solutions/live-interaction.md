## What this capability is

Live interaction is not one-way distribution. It is the product capability for sustained room-based interaction among hosts, guests, and audiences.

## Common scenarios

- voice rooms
- showroom-style live products
- 1v1 private sessions
- high-frequency interactive live workloads

## What to read first

If the product depends on in-room participation and co-speaking, start here before looking at pure distribution flows.
