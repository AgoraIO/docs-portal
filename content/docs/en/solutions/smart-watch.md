## What this solution is

Smart Watch is the device solution for wearable communication products. It helps teams think in terms of watch hardware, device constraints, and communication workflows rather than generic app-first RTC integration.

## Best fit

- watch-to-watch communication
- watch-to-phone communication
- wearable audio or video call products

## Built on

- device-side media transport
- realtime audio and video communication
- device-and-client interoperability

## Choose this when

Choose this path when the main product is a wearable endpoint and the experience must be designed around device constraints and communication workflows.
