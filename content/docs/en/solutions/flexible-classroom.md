## What this solution is

Flexible Classroom is Agora's low-code classroom product path. It is the right entry when you want a classroom product with more built-in structure than raw RTC, RTM, whiteboard, and recording integration.

## Best fit

- teams that want faster classroom launch
- interactive teaching products with multiple class formats
- education products that prefer a packaged classroom experience over custom orchestration

## Built on

- realtime audio and video
- messaging and classroom coordination
- whiteboard and recording

## Choose this when

Choose Flexible Classroom when your product is clearly an education product and you want the classroom mental model to be the primary navigation frame.
