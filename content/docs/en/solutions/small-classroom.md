## What this solution is

Small Classroom is the education solution for one-to-many interactive teaching products. It fits teams that want to design their own teaching workflow for small-group classes instead of adopting a more packaged classroom product.

## Best fit

- one-to-many online classes
- tutoring groups
- seminar-style interactive teaching

## Built on

- realtime audio and video
- messaging and classroom state coordination
- whiteboard and recording for class review

## Choose this when

Choose this path when your product is fundamentally a small-group class and you need more customization than a low-code classroom product provides.
