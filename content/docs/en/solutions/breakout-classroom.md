## What this solution is

Breakout Classroom is the education solution for products that combine large-class teaching with small-group discussion. It helps teams model both the main teaching session and the breakout structure as part of one teaching system.

## Best fit

- large interactive classes
- live teaching plus group discussion
- classroom products with teachers, assistants, and student subgroups

## Built on

- realtime audio and video
- classroom coordination across main rooms and subrooms
- whiteboard, recording, and teaching workflow integration

## Choose this when

Choose this path when the product needs both a main classroom and breakout-group collaboration instead of a single flat class structure.
