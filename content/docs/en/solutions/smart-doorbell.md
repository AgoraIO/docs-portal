## What this solution is

Smart Doorbell is the device solution for entrance-security style products. It combines device access, realtime communication, and cloud-side coordination into a more recognizable product path for doorbell-class devices.

## Best fit

- visual doorbells
- video door locks
- smart peepholes

## Built on

- device-side media transport
- app-side realtime audio and video
- signaling, notifications, and device control

## Choose this when

Choose this path when the product is centered on visitor communication and entry-device coordination rather than on a general-purpose camera workflow.
