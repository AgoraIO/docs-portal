## What this solution is

Flexible Meeting is Agora's packaged meeting product path. It is the right entry when you need a recognizable meeting experience instead of assembling raw RTC, signaling, recording, and collaboration capabilities one by one.

## Best fit

- enterprise meetings
- team collaboration
- structured multi-party sessions
- products that need room, participant, and meeting-control concepts out of the box

## Built on

- realtime audio and video
- meeting orchestration and backend integration
- optional whiteboard, recording, and other collaboration add-ons

## Choose this when

Choose Flexible Meeting when your users think in terms of meetings, hosts, attendees, and collaboration workflows, not just generic audio-video channels.
