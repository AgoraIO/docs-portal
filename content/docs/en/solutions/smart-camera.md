## What this solution is

Smart Camera is the device solution for products such as IP cameras and similar always-on visual devices. It organizes the docs around the device product itself rather than around individual RTC, RTSA, or RTM capabilities.

## Best fit

- IP camera products
- visual monitoring devices
- device-to-app products that need both media and control

## Built on

- device-side media transport
- client-side realtime audio and video
- signaling for state and control

## Choose this when

Choose this path when the product is clearly a camera-class device and the main design question is how the device and client work together as one system.
