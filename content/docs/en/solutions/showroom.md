## What this solution is

Showroom Live is Agora's entertainment live solution for products built around hosts, audiences, co-host interaction, and room-based engagement. It is closer to a monetized live-show product than to a generic RTC room.

## Best fit

- single-host live rooms
- audience-to-host co-speaking
- multi-host PK or cross-room interaction
- entertainment products that need a stronger live-show mental model than meetings or classrooms

## Built on

- realtime audio and video
- room interaction and audience participation
- live-show oriented product flows and moderation surfaces

## Choose this when

Choose Showroom Live when your product is fundamentally an entertainment broadcast with interactive participation instead of a meeting or a simple call.
