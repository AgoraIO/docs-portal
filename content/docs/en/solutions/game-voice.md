## What this solution is

Game Voice is Agora's packaged in-game voice solution. It is designed for teams building game communication experiences, where voice quality, low friction, moderation, and cross-platform compatibility matter more than generic conferencing concepts.

## Best fit

- mobile game voice
- PC and browser game voice
- squad, team, or guild communication
- game products that need embedded voice plus moderation and recording-related capabilities

## Built on

- low-latency realtime voice
- game-oriented audio tuning
- supporting services such as recording and content-safety workflows

## Choose this when

Choose Game Voice when voice is part of gameplay and team coordination, rather than a standalone media product.
