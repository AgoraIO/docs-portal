## What this solution is

Art Class is a specialized education solution for visual-art teaching. It is built for products where camera correction, image fidelity, dual-camera support, and artwork presentation quality are part of the core teaching experience.

## Best fit

- drawing instruction
- art exam prep
- studio-style remote teaching
- products that need better visual restoration than a generic classroom setup

## Built on

- realtime audio and video
- whiteboard and classroom coordination
- camera correction and visual-teaching oriented enhancements

## Choose this when

Choose Art Class when the central teaching challenge is visual demonstration quality rather than only generic classroom interaction.
