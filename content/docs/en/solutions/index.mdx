Solutions is the scenario domain for developers who already know the kind of product they want to launch, but do not want to assemble the underlying realtime stack from scratch. Use this section when your main question is "what experience am I building?" rather than "which media capability do I need first?"

This tab is organized by product pattern and business workflow rather than by protocol, API, or infrastructure layer. Each solution path pulls together the capabilities, roles, interaction model, and implementation scope for a recognizable product shape such as live streaming, gaming voice, meetings, classrooms, or connected devices.

Use this overview to understand what kinds of product guides exist in the Solutions tab, how those guides are grouped, where different topics live, and how the sections relate before you move into the individual solution guides.

## How content is grouped

The groups below outline the main product patterns in this tab. Read them as a map of the Solutions content first, then go deeper into the related guides for architecture, scope, and implementation details.

## What each group covers

### Live interaction products

This group covers products centered on people joining a shared live session and interacting in real time.

- [Enable live streaming](/en/solutions/showroom): host-audience rooms, co-hosting, PK, and live room engagement flows
- [Add in-game voice](/en/solutions/game-voice): low-friction team voice, squad coordination, and game-native voice presence
- [Launch meetings](/en/solutions/meeting): structured multi-party collaboration with participant control and meeting workflows
- [Enable lightweight calling](/en/solutions/voip-call): simple realtime calling for mini-programs, terminals, wearables, and lightweight clients

### Online classrooms

This group covers teaching workflows with teacher and student roles, classroom orchestration, and lesson-specific interaction patterns.

- [Run online classrooms](/en/solutions/flexible-classroom): a general classroom path for teaching-first experiences
- [Teach 1-on-1](/en/solutions/one-to-one-classroom): direct teacher-student sessions
- [Teach small groups](/en/solutions/small-classroom): small group interactive teaching
- [Break out large classes](/en/solutions/breakout-classroom): large classes with breakout discussion
- [Teach art online](/en/solutions/art-class): visual teaching with camera fidelity
- [Teach music online](/en/solutions/online-music-class): music lessons with audio-first interaction

### Connected devices

This group covers products where realtime interaction is attached to a device, operator console, embedded endpoint, or remote control workflow.

- [Operate devices remotely](/en/solutions/teleoperation): realtime control and operator feedback workflows
- [Connect smart cameras](/en/solutions/smart-camera): camera-to-app media connectivity
- [Connect smart doorbells](/en/solutions/smart-doorbell): doorbell and lock communication paths
- [Enable watch calling](/en/solutions/smart-watch): compact calling on wearable devices
