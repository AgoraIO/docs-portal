## What this capability is

Education-classroom products are not one isolated technology. They combine RTC, whiteboard, captions, collaboration, and class-management flows into a teaching-focused system.

## Common scenarios

- one-to-one classes
- small classes
- breakout-style classrooms
- meeting-classroom hybrids

## What to read first

If the goal is teaching rather than generic meetings, start from the classroom family as a combined capability view before drilling into RTC, whiteboard, or transcription.
