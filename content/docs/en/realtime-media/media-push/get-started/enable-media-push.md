---
title: "Enable Media Push"
description: "Shows how to enable Media Push."
---

Media Push enables you to stream audio and video from Agora channels to CDNs and other RTMP-based platforms. To enable Media Push, in [Agora Console](https://console.agora.io), select **Project Settings** from the sidebar, switch to the **Services** tab and find **Media Push**. Turn on the **Active** toggle. Read the pop-up prompt carefully, and then click **Confirm** to enable.

:::info
You cannot disable the service once you enable it.
:::

To process a media stream and push it to a CDN, you create a converter in the Agora channel. To create and manage converters, see [Use RESTful API](../build/restful-api).
