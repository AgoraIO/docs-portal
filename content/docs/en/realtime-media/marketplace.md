## What this capability is

Marketplace helps teams discover, purchase, and integrate modules or extension capabilities that fit their realtime workloads.

## Common scenarios

- faster capability selection
- trying or purchasing extension modules
- account and capability integration

## What to read first

If the key question is "what can we plug in quickly" instead of "what do we build from scratch," Marketplace is the better entry point.
