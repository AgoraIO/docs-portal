After you [enable Chat](/en/realtime-media/im/get-started/enable#enable-chat) for a project, use Agora Console to turn on Chat features and manage your Chat plan.

## Enable Chat features

To turn on Chat features:

1. In [Agora Console](https://console.agora.io/), select your project from the **Project** list at the top of the page.

1. In the sidebar, click **RTC Services**, then click **Open Chat Console** on the **Chat** card.

1. In the Chat Console sidebar, click **Features Overview**.

1. Turn on the features that you need. Features that show **Upgrade Your Plan** aren't available on your current plan. To upgrade, see [Change the Chat plan](#change-the-chat-plan).

   ![Chat Console Features Overview page](/temp/chat-console/chat-features-overview.png)

To set up offline push notifications, see [Offline push overview](/en/realtime-media/im/build/notifications-and-event-handling/offline-push/overview).

## Change the Chat plan

New Chat projects use the Free plan. To change your plan:

1. In the [Agora Console](https://console.agora.io/) sidebar, click **Subscriptions**, then select the **Chat** tab.

1. Click **Change plan** on the plan that you want. For the Enterprise plan, click **Contact sales**.

:::note
- The plan change takes effect immediately.
- Agora doesn't recommend downgrading your plan, as it could impact the capacity of your applications and availability of certain features.
:::

For a comparison of plans, see [Pricing plan details](/en/realtime-media/im/reference/pricing-plan-details).

## Disable Chat

You can't disable Chat for a project in Agora Console. To disable Chat, [contact technical support](mailto:support@agora.io).

## Unsubscribe from Chat

To unsubscribe from a paid plan, downgrade to the Free plan:

1. In the [Agora Console](https://console.agora.io/) sidebar, click **Subscriptions**, then select the **Chat** tab.

1. Click **Change plan** on the **Free** plan.

The Free plan has lower limits and fewer features. For a comparison of plans, see [Pricing plan details](/en/realtime-media/im/reference/pricing-plan-details).

## Monitor Chat usage

After you enable Chat, the Chat-related features in Agora Analytics are enabled by default to help you keep track of usage trends and quality details. For more information, see [Data Insights](/en/realtime-media/im/reference/console/data-insights) and [Data Metrics](/en/realtime-media/im/reference/console/data-metrics).

To monitor real-time activity, message delivery, user connections, and device logs in Chat Console, see [Monitor Chat usage and quality](/en/realtime-media/im/reference/console/real-time-query).
