## What this capability is

IM is designed for fuller chat systems rather than only room-level realtime messaging. It usually covers conversation models, contacts, message history, and large-scale messaging service behavior.

## Common scenarios

- in-app chat
- social interaction
- customer messaging systems
- persistent messaging infrastructure

## Current source-backed position

In the current Shengwang product structure, IM sits beside RTM. RTM is better for realtime state and signaling, while IM fits full messaging systems.

## What to read first

If your target is a chat product rather than a room-level coordination layer, IM should be evaluated before RTM.
