---
title: "What products does Agora provide?"
---

Agora is a Platform as a Service (PaaS) provider that enables real-time communicatons with easy-to-embed SDKs and RESTful APIs. Through calling the APIs, you can add the following functions in your applications:

- [Video Calling](/en/video-calling/overview/product-overview)
- [Interactive Live Streaming](/en/interactive-live-streaming/overview/product-overview)
- [Voice Calling](/en/voice-calling/overview/product-overview)
- [Chat (Beta)](/en/agora-chat/overview/product-overview)
- [Signaling](/en/signaling/overview/product-overview)
- [Interactive Whiteboard](/en/interactive-whiteboard/overview/product-overview)
- [Analytics](/en/agora-analytics/overview/product-overview)
- [Cloud Recording](/en/cloud-recording/overview/product-overview)
- [On-Premise Recording](/en/on-premise-recording/overview/product-overview)
- [Server Gateway](/en/server-gateway/overview/product-overview)
- [Flexible classroom](/en/flexible-classroom/overview/product-overview)
- [Media Push](/en/media-push/overview/product-overview)
- [Media Pull](/en/media-pull/overview/product-overview)
