Convo AI Device Kit depends on Conversational AI and Agora IoT capabilities. Before running the kit or demo projects, make sure the relevant services are enabled for your Agora project.

## Required setup

1. Sign in to the [Agora Console](https://console.agora.io).
2. Open the target project in **Projects**.
3. Enable **Conversational AI** for the project.
4. Prepare the credentials needed by your device or demo environment.
