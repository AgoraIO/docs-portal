title: TTS models
description: Choose a text-to-speech provider for Voice Agent.

TTS converts agent responses into audio output. When you choose a TTS provider, compare voice quality, streaming first-byte latency, language and voice coverage, regional or account requirements, and support for the sample rates or streaming protocols your app needs.
