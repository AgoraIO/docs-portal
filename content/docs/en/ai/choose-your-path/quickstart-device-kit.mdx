Choose this path if you are building a hardware product and need a packaged route for putting a voice agent on a device instead of assembling the full stack yourself.

This path opens the Convo AI Device Kit product space, where the docs are organized around hardware bring-up, device setup, firmware integration, and rollout planning.

## Start here

- [Open Convo AI Device Kit](../device-kit/index.md)

## Best fit

- You are building AI toys, companions, wearables, or home devices.
- You need to deploy a voice agent onto dedicated hardware.
- You want hardware, connectivity, and voice AI resources bundled into one path.
