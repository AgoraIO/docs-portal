Multimodal large language models (MLLMs) can process realtime audio and text in a single model path. Use this option when you want to reduce the number of separate ASR, LLM, and TTS components in the conversation pipeline.

## Providers

- [OpenAI Realtime](openai.md)
- [Google Gemini Live](gemini.md)
- [Google Vertex AI](google-vertex-ai.md)
- [xAI Grok](xai.md)
