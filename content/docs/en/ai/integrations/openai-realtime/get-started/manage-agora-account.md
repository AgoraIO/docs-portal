This page shows you how to prepare an Agora account and project before working with the OpenAI Realtime integration path.

## What you need

- An Agora account
- A project in Agora Console
- App ID and related credentials for your chosen integration pattern
- Service enablement that matches your target product path

## Basic flow

1. Sign in to the [Agora Console](https://console.agora.io).
2. Create a project for your integration environment.
3. Collect the App ID and any server-side credentials you need.
4. Enable the services required for your integration path.
