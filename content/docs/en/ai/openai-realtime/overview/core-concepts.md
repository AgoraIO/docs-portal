Combining Agora's realtime audio communication with OpenAI's large language models opens up a voice-first integration path for interactive applications.

## General concepts

- **App ID**: the unique identifier for your Agora project.
- **App certificate**: the server-side credential used in token-related flows.
- **Token**: the authentication credential used when users or services join channels.
- **Channel**: the live context that groups participants and media streams.
- **User ID**: the identifier used for clients, services, or agents in a session.
- **SD-RTN**: Agora's realtime transport network.
- **Agora Console**: where you create projects, enable services, and manage credentials.

## RESTful APIs

Agora offers RESTful APIs across many products. In this integration path, they are typically used to control setup, backend flows, or product orchestration around the realtime audio session.
