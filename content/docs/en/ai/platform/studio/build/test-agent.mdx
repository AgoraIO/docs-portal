Use the **Test agent** panel to confirm that your selected models and integrations work together as expected, and to refine your prompts and provider settings before you deploy.

## Run a test

To talk to your agent:

1. In the navigation sidebar, select **Agents**, then select the agent you want to test. The **Test agent** panel appears next to the agent editor, and also on the agent's overview page.
2. If your prompt uses [variables](/en/ai/platform/studio/build/prompt-design#template-variables), enter their values. This previews the values without saving or deploying them.
3. Select **Start test**, and allow microphone access when prompted.

    ![Test agent panel during a test, with the session ID, transcript, and latency](/temp/agent-studio-preview/test-session.png)

4. Speak to your agent, and confirm that it responds correctly and maintains a conversation without errors.
5. Select **End test** to stop the session.

:::warning
Always select **End test** to stop a test. Closing the **Test agent** panel only releases your browser's audio.
:::

During a test, the panel shows the following:

- **Status**: What the agent is doing, such as **Speaking**.
- **Session ID**: The ID of this session. Use it to find the session in [Agent Analytics](/en/ai/platform/studio/observe/agent-analytics).
- **Transcript**: The conversation so far.
- **Turn latency**: The latency of the conversation, in milliseconds.
- **Controls**: Buttons to mute your microphone and show or hide captions.

The panel tests your saved configuration. In the agent editor, your changes save automatically. In [Advanced settings](/en/ai/platform/studio/build/customize-agent#advanced-settings), select **Save** first.

Test sessions appear on the agent's [overview page](/en/ai/platform/studio/observe/agent-overview) with the **App** channel.

## What to test

During testing, verify the following:

- **ASR accuracy**: Speak clearly and check whether the agent correctly understands your speech. Review the transcript to verify transcription accuracy. If the agent frequently misunderstands you, try a different ASR provider.
- **LLM responses**: Confirm that the agent responds appropriately to your queries. If responses are off-topic or unclear, refine your system prompt.
- **TTS quality**: Listen to the agent's voice. If it sounds unnatural or has pronunciation issues, select a different voice or provider.
- **Conversation flow**: Test multi-turn conversations to ensure that the agent maintains context and handles follow-up questions correctly.
- **Integrations**: If you added an MCP server or a tool, trigger a conversation that invokes it, and verify that the agent calls it correctly.

## Data retention

For an Inbound or Outbound agent, the **Retention** section of the **Deploy agent** tab controls whether Agora keeps a test session's conversation data. Turn off **Keep transcripts and recordings for 30 days** to discard the data. This setting applies to your next test session, and doesn't change retention for deployed calls.

For API sessions, you set retention when you start each session. See [Choose whether Agora retains conversation data](/en/ai/platform/build/shape-the-conversation/personalize-conversations#choose-whether-agora-retains-conversation-data).

## Troubleshooting and debugging

If you encounter connectivity or quality issues, refer to the following troubleshooting guide.

### Connection failures

If you can't connect to the agent:

- Check your internet connection.
- Refresh the page and try again.
- If you use your own keys, verify that they're valid.

### Agent doesn't respond

If the agent doesn't respond after you speak:

- Verify that your microphone is on, and that your browser has permission to use it.
- If you use your own keys, ensure that they're active and have sufficient quota with your providers.

### Poor speech recognition

If the agent frequently misunderstands your speech:

- Speak clearly and reduce background noise.
- Check that the ASR **Language** setting matches the language you're speaking.
- Try a different model or provider.

### Out-of-context responses

If the agent provides irrelevant or unexpected responses:

- Review and refine your [system prompt](/en/ai/platform/studio/build/prompt-design).
- Check that the selected LLM is appropriate for your use case.
- Verify that your greeting sets the proper context for the conversation.

### Voice quality issues

If the agent's voice sounds distorted or unnatural:

- Check your audio output device.
- Select a different voice or provider.
