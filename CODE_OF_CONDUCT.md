# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free collaboration space for
everyone contributing to this documentation project.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus feedback on the content, code, and technical outcome.
- Give enough context for maintainers and contributors to reproduce or evaluate
  a report.
- Respect privacy and confidentiality. Do not post secrets, customer data, or
  private support details.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Repeated off-topic, disruptive, or hostile comments.
- Intentionally misleading reports or pull requests.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and may restrict participation
when behavior does not meet this code of conduct. Serious security or abuse
issues should be reported through the appropriate private channel.
